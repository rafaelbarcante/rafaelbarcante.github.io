Project Overview

Bushido Saga is a mobile action game where the player controls a samurai who slashes enemies by swiping the screen and advances through a storyline across multiple levels.

I worked with Pandora Game Studio designing and implementing a new Level Design approach.

Key Results

  • Changed the level design approach, boosting Retention and Session Time

Level Design

My work as a Level Designer helped the team replace its previous approach. My focus was to move from an open, exploratory design to a more straightforward one, with a clear main path and slight deviations with secret rewards.

Below are the new levels I built for Bushido Saga using preexisting assets, which appear in the later segments of the game. Levels 1, 2 and 3 were adjusted to this new approach by the rest of the team.
