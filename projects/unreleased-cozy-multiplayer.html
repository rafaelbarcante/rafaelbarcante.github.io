Unreleased Online Multiplayer Cozy Game

  • StudioFortis Games
  • RoleSenior Game Designer
  • PlatformMobile

Project Overview

An online multiplayer cozy game with F2P monetization.

Players develop their own plot of land in a dynamic neighborhood, where their neighbors change depending on who’s online.

Features include farming, crafting, fishing, and a strong focus on raising and breeding pets.

My Responsibilities

  • Reported to the Game Director, leading small, rotating pods assembled for specific features.
  • Designed and balanced different gameplay systems and their monetized progression.

Key Results

  • Led the design of several features of a large-scale online multiplayer game.
  • Each feature was developed in a rotating pod where I was the designer.
